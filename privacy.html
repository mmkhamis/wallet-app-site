Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wallet ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you register or sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Financial data: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, and notes that you manually enter or import.
  • Device information: Device type and OS version, used only for compatibility purposes.
  • Usage data: App feature usage for improving the product (anonymized).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality.
  • To personalize your financial insights and AI assistant responses.
  • To send important notifications about your account (no marketing emails without consent).
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on AWS infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. Sensitive credentials are stored using device-level secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). We never store your banking passwords or card numbers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method
  • Apple Sign-In — optional authentication method (iOS only)
  • OpenAI — AI-powered financial insights (data is anonymized before sending)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your financial data with advertisers. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above, strictly to operate the App.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from the App's settings screen. Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days.

7. SMS Data (Android Only)

On Android, the App may request permission to read SMS messages to automatically detect bank transaction notifications. SMS data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers without your explicit approval of each transaction.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at support@walletapp.io to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
support@walletapp.io
